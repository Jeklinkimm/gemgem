Partner trial privacy

Updated October 13, 2026.

What you provide

The form collects your name, email, organization, country, intended device and readiness to start. It also records the submission time and campaign tags. Please use adult contact details and do not include child names or health records.

How we use it

GemGem Therapeutics uses these details to respond to your trial request, check your setup, coordinate the evaluation agreement and account access, and arrange feedback. A request does not itself sign an agreement or activate an account.

Services

GitHub Pages hosts this page. The existing Google Apps Script and Google Sheets process and store requests. The existing notification system sends your name, email and submission time to the GemGem team's Discord channel. If you choose the optional call link after asking questions, Calendly processes the booking under its privacy notice. This form does not send your answers to an analytics service or store them in browser storage.

Retention and contact

We retain inquiry information for follow-up and remove it when it is no longer needed or when you request deletion. To request access, correction, deletion or an end to follow-up, email jeklinkim@gemgem.me.
