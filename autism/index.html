What autism program teams want to know

Can we lower sound or visual intensity?

The group leader needs separate controls for sound and visual effects before and during the activity.

Can a child watch, step out, and rejoin?

Participation should not depend on staying in the camera frame for the whole activity.

Will one child be singled out?

The concept uses shared progress and does not put individual scores on display.

Can children participate in different ways?

Standing, seated, smaller, and alternate movements remain design requirements to test with the program.

What still needs to be tested
