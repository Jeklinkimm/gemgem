Inquiry privacy notice

This notice explains how information from the GemGem400 inquiry page is used.

Information you provide

The form collects your name, email, professional role, organization, intended setting, and requested next step. It also includes submission time and campaign tags from the page link. Please provide adult contact information only, with no child names, patient records, or other personal health information.

How we use it

We use your submission to respond to your inquiry, share requested information, and discuss a demo or potential fit for your program. Submitting this form does not enroll you in a clinical study or commit you to a purchase or pilot.

Services used by this page

GitHub Pages hosts this page. Google Apps Script and Google Sheets process and store inquiry submissions. Mixpanel records page views and form interaction events, along with campaign tags and browser/device metadata, to help us understand how the page is used. This form does not send your name, email, organization, or answers to Mixpanel. Mixpanel may store a browser identifier in local storage. We do not enable session recording on this page.

Retention and requests

We retain inquiry information for follow-up and remove it when it is no longer needed or when you request deletion. To request access, correction, deletion, or an end to follow-up, email hello@gemgem.me.
