Booking privacy notice

This notice explains how information from the GemGem400 landing page is used.

Information you provide

This landing page has no contact form. An embedded Calendly calendar lets you select a time and provide your name and email without leaving this page. Please provide adult contact information only, with no child names, patient records, or other personal health information.

How we use it

We use your booking information to respond to your inquiry, share requested information, and discuss a demo or potential fit for your program. Booking a meeting does not enroll you in a clinical study or commit you to a purchase or pilot.

Services used by this page

GitHub Pages hosts this page. Calendly loads its booking interface on this page and processes meeting bookings under its privacy notice. Mixpanel records page views and booking-link clicks, along with campaign tags and browser/device metadata, to help us understand how the page is used. We do not send booking details to Mixpanel. Mixpanel may store a browser identifier in local storage. We do not enable session recording on this page.

Retention and requests

We retain inquiry information for follow-up and remove it when it is no longer needed or when you request deletion. To request access, correction, deletion, or an end to follow-up, email hello@gemgem.me.
