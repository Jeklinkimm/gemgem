PUBLISHED CP STUDY

45 children · 8 weeks · 3 × 15 min/week

Open the paper on PubMed open_in_new

Promising study results; not proof for every child, setting, device, or outcome.

Study details and limitsexpand_more
DOI: 10.1080/09638288.2026.2735445

Population: 45 children with cerebral palsy, aged 18 months to 7 years 5 months.

Program: Randomized controlled trial; 8 weeks; three 15-minute sessions per week.

Reported result: Significant time-by-group effects for Box and Block Test and palmar pinch strength in both hands, and for Functional Dexterity Test and tip pinch strength in the non-dominant hand.

What this study does not tell us: This study does not establish results for every diagnosis, age, movement profile, device setup, institution, or a one-month evaluation. Digital practice complements—not replaces—clinician-led care.

Questions provider teams ask before trying GemGem400.

Can we use an iPad or another institution-owned device?expand_more

The current app is built for iOS and Android devices with a front-facing camera. Bring the exact device model and OS version so compatibility and IT approval steps can be confirmed before an evaluation.

What about left-hand, right-hand, or bilateral use?expand_more

You can set GemGem400 to track the left hand, the right hand, or both hands depending on the child and session goal.

Will it recognize every hand shape or range of motion?expand_more

Recognition varies by child, involuntary movement, and camera position. You can choose from four recognition grades (Easy, Good, Great, Perfect) to test fit.

What video or movement data is stored or transmitted?expand_more

The front-facing camera detects hand landmarks on the local device during gameplay. We review exact data flow, storage, and privacy documentation with each team before any evaluation.

Can we export data or connect it to a research system?expand_more

Standard clinic use does not require data export. Research or institutional integrations can be discussed during an intro call.
